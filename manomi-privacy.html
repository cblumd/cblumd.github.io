Privacy Policy for Manomi Flashcards

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

Manomi Flashcards ("the App") is developed and operated by Christopher Blumhagen ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the App.

Summary: Manomi Flashcards is an offline-first flashcard application. We do not maintain user accounts, run tracking servers, or sell your personal data. Your decks and learning progress remain locally stored on your device. Third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) may collect standard device identifiers to display relevant ads and handle European regulatory consent.

1. Offline-First Data Storage

All flashcard content created or imported by the user—including custom decks, cards, folders, tags, study logs, spaced repetition intervals, and learning statistics—is stored solely on your local device. We do not have access to, nor do we host or synchronize, your personal study decks on proprietary external servers.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display in-app advertisements (such as banners and rewarded ads). To serve ads, prevent fraud, and fulfill legal compliance, AdMob may collect and process certain technical data from your device, including:

  • Advertising IDs (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • Diagnostic metrics, crash reports, and app performance data
  • Ad interaction and engagement statistics

For more information on how Google manages advertising data, visit the Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. European Regulations & User Consent (GDPR / UK GDPR)

For users located within the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App integrates Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent Management Platform (CMP). This framework presents users with explicit choices regarding cookies, personalized advertising, and partner data processing before ads are delivered. You can review or modify your consent choices at any time within the App's settings.

4. Photos and Media Access

When you customize flashcard backgrounds using your own images, the App utilizes standard Android system file and photo pickers. The App only accesses the individual image files you deliberately choose and select. It does not scan, index, read, or upload your wider photo library.

5. Children’s Privacy

Manomi Flashcards is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to third-party services via the App, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, technical improvements, or changing legal obligations. Updates will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
