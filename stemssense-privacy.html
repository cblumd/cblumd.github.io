Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026
Application: StemsSense (and affiliated utility tools)

This Privacy Policy explains how our applications handle your data. We design our software with a local-first, privacy-respecting architecture: your core data belongs to you and stays on your device.

1. Offline-First Core Architecture

  • Local Data Storage: All botanical logs, plant images, soil metrics, schedules, and custom journal entries are stored locally on your device storage (via indexed local database and local filesystem).
  • Zero Cloud Synchronization: We do not host central database servers holding your personal plant notes, photos, or location details.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide an email address or phone number to use the application.

2. Telemetry and Analytics (Google Firebase)

To help us diagnose technical issues, optimize performance, and understand high-level feature adoption, StemsSense integrates Google Firebase Analytics (provided by Google LLC).

What is collected:

  • App Activity: Anonymized, aggregated usage events (e.g., feature clicks, screen views, care event confirmations, discrete species names, and broad agronomic categories).
  • Device & Diagnostic Identifiers: Pseudonymous identifiers (such as Firebase App Instance ID, Android OS version, device model, and general hardware performance).
  • Crash & Health Diagnostics: Technical error logs and crash stack traces to help us resolve software bugs.
  • Coarse Geolocation: Non-precise, country- or city-level location derived automatically from your IP address by Google servers.

What is NEVER collected:

  • We never transmit free-form user notes, journal entries, personal names, phone contacts, exact GPS micro-coordinates, or raw camera photographs to analytics servers.

Google processes this information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all botanical data is stored strictly on your device, deleting your app data or uninstalling the application permanently removes all your personal botanical records immediately.

Aggregated, non-identifying telemetry retained by Google Firebase is subject to standard automated retention periods (typically 2 to 14 months) before automated purging.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our applications do not address or intentionally collect personal identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our software practices, please reach out via GitHub or contact the developer directly.
